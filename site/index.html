06 · Severity from blood

The number that changed when we looked closer

The paper reports 98% accuracy for Extra Trees. We reproduce it — then show it depends on how patients are split.

Key finding

Same models. Same data.

The dataset holds rows from only patients. When SMOTE-ENN is applied to all rows and cross-validation then splits rows, the same patient (and synthetic neighbours of them) lands in both training and test. Keeping every patient on one side of the split, and balancing only inside training folds, gives the honest picture.
